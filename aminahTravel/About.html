About Aminah Travels

Who are We

Making the Best Umrah Experience

In 2016 we started Amina travelers with an objective to make the Hajj and Umrah industry more friendly and accessible to the Muslims of the world. With our efficient network, we can compare a wide network of travel suppliers, airlines, and hotels to make the best Umrah and Hajj packages that suit your needs and budget. With over two decades of experience operating as a travel company in this sector, we are providing our customers with impressive and state-of-the-art guidance to make their blessed journey as amicable as possible. Throughout this journey, we were able to facilitate trips for over a thousand satisfied customers who enjoyed our services with positive feedback, which helps us progress and develop a strong reputation in this industry. In addition to these holy trips for Hajj and Umrah, we also offer 'halal' trips to Spain, Turkey, Morocco, and Jordan as well as guided tours Masjid-al-Aqsa.

Making the Best Umrah Experience

We call ourselves experts in Hajj and Umrah and other Islamic tours and our professional team will guide you at each step and devise a plan according to your need to make your trip a remarkable experience for you.

We make sure that we provide the best facilities and deals in every Hajj and Umrah pilgrim and by the grace of Almighty Allah we are happy to uphold our promises along with maintaining the trust of our customers. We make sure to deliver you as promised and eliminate any doubts in your mind. By nature, the Hajj and Umrah pilgrimage is not like your ordinary vacation so it is vital to be transparent and precise while dealing in this regard and communicate one on one to ensure that both parties are on the same page.

In addition, we have detailed categories of Hajj and Umrah deals ranging from budget to luxury and you'll find full discretion of each deal. We have encountered many complaints by other companies, for instance, saying the hotel would be at a walking distance from the main mosque in Makkah or Madina while in reality, it takes over 15 minutes which is exhausting for some clients. We believe in transparency and telling our customers the truth rather than following the footsteps of our competitors and customers are delighted with honesty as it is the main motive of our company. Integrity and fairness are what you find here at aminatravels.co.uk.
